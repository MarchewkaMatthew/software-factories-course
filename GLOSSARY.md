# Cadence

An online course platform: Instructors publish Courses, Students buy or are given access to them and work through Lessons.

## People and roles

**User**:
An account on the platform. Every Student, Instructor and Admin is a User.
_Avoid_: Account, person, someone

**Student**:
A User who takes Courses.
_Avoid_: Learner, buyer, purchaser, customer

**Instructor**:
A User with the role that can create and teach Courses.
_Avoid_: Teacher, author, creator, seller

**Course Instructor**:
The Instructor who owns a particular Course.
_Avoid_: Owner, owning instructor

**Admin**:
A User who runs the whole platform. Always means the platform role; never the team role.
_Avoid_: Administrator, superuser

**Staff**:
The Course Instructor of a Course, or an Admin — the people who answer for that Course.

## Courses

**Course**:
A published body of teaching, made of Modules, that a Student can enroll in.

**Module**:
An ordered group of Lessons within a Course.
_Avoid_: Section, chapter, unit

**Lesson**:
A single unit of teaching within a Module, usually a video and optionally a Quiz.
_Avoid_: Lecture, video

**Course Content**:
The full outline of a Course's Modules and Lessons.
_Avoid_: Curriculum, syllabus

**Category**:
A label grouping Courses for browsing.
_Avoid_: Tag, topic, subject

## Buying and access

**Purchase**:
A payment for a Course, made by a Student for themselves or by a Team Admin for Seats.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction, buying (as a noun)

**Enrollment**:
A Student's access to a Course, whether it came from a Purchase or from redeeming a Coupon.
_Avoid_: Enrolment, access, joining

**PPP Discount**:
A Purchasing Power Parity price reduction for Students in lower-income countries, set by the Student's country.
_Avoid_: Parity discount, parity pricing, regional pricing, location-based discount

**PPP Tier**:
The band of countries sharing one PPP Discount.

## Teams

**Team**:
A group of Users who share Seats bought by their Team Admin.

**Team Admin**:
The Team member who buys Seats and hands out their Coupons. Always qualified with "Team".
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), team owner

**Seat**:
One person's place in a Course, bought by a Team Admin as part of a Purchase.
_Avoid_: License, slot

**Coupon**:
The code, and the link carrying it, that hands one Seat to whoever redeems it.
_Avoid_: Redemption link, invite, voucher

**Redeem**:
To use a Coupon and take its Seat. A Coupon is either Redeemed or Unredeemed.
_Avoid_: Claim, use, accept

## Refunds

**Refund**:
A Student's request to get their money back for a Purchase. A Refund is Requested, then Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, reversal, cancellation, cancel purchase

**Refund Amount**:
What a Refund returns: always the amount the Student paid for the Purchase.

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund can be requested.

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase can still be refunded: inside the Refund Window, not too much of the Course watched or completed, not a Redeemed Seat, and no Refund already Requested.

## Learning

**Progress**:
How far a Student has got through a Lesson or Course: Not Started, In Progress, or Completed.

**Completed**:
The state of a Lesson the Student has marked done, or of a Course whose every Lesson is Completed.
_Avoid_: Finished, done

**Quiz**:
A set of Questions attached to a Lesson.

**Question**:
One item in a Quiz. Never a Comment.

**Rating**:
A Student's one-to-five star score for a Course.
_Avoid_: Review

## Discussion

**Comment**:
A post by a User under a Lesson, or a reply to another Comment.
_Avoid_: Question, discussion post

**Unanswered Comment**:
A top-level Comment that no Staff has replied to yet.
_Avoid_: Unanswered question
